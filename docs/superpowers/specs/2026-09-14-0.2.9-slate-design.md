## 0.9.0 flagship spun off: Recipes (#220)

Survey + architecture-gap analysis (2026-09-14, three research lanes) concluded in [#220 — Recipes: repeatable multi-page workflows as a first-class primitive (human-checkpointed)](https://github.com/LogicIncZo/zo-cobrowse/issues/220). Origin requirement (owner): payment can't be automated → automation must be human-in-loop by design; extension-driven choreography around human-owned trust steps is the co-browse form factor. R1 (schema + deterministic player + `human` checkpoints, machine-checked no-auto-submit), R2 (authoring from runs + healer self-heal), R3 (library + SKILL.md-compatible share via the skills picker). Worked example: RTI filing on rtionline.gov.in (guest flow: guidelines → captcha+OTP **human** → form fill → captcha+SBI-payment **human** → extract registration number).

**Why not 0.2.9:** honest sizing — R1 alone is Lane-E scale; 0.2.9's four items are deliberate substrates (#52 pull-loop maturation, #51/#54 serializer + run-persistence patterns).

## Release mechanics

1. All four items merge to `dev` (CI + e2e green per PR; findings queue stays empty — `qa-gate`).
2. `chore(release)`: manifest+package → `0.2.9.0`, CHANGELOG stamp → dev → dev→main promotion PR (**merge commit, never squash**) → tag `v0.2.9.0` → `release.yml` publishes.
3. Manual-panel checklist pass before promotion (standing pre-promotion gate).
